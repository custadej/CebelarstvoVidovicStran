About us

With bees since primary school.

I learned beekeeping from the beekeeper Janko Pislak, where I worked full time from 1998. In 2014 I started on my own, with my first 60 hives and a mobile apiary we built at home.

Today our apiaries stand at home on Dravsko polje, in Haloze and in Prekmurje. Family and friends help with the work.
