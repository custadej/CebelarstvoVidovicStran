Purpose of the site

The site presents the beekeeping business and its products. It is not an online shop: you cannot place a binding order or pay through it. We agree on price, availability, collection and delivery by phone or e-mail.

Content

Product descriptions are for information. Bee products are foods, not medicines; the descriptions do not replace the advice of a doctor or pharmacist. We do our best to keep the information accurate, but what we offer can change during the season.

Awards and reviews

The awards listed are documented with certificates from the organisers of the evaluations. Customer reviews are taken from public Google ratings and from messages we received; they are given with the first name and the initial of the surname. On the English and German pages they are translated from Slovenian.
