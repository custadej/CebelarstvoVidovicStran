Bee products

Everything the hive gives.

Besides honey, you can get fresh royal jelly, bee pollen, propolis, beeswax items and gift boxes from us.

Call Jože · +386 41 579 827

  • Royal jelly

    Fresh, straight from the hive. We have produced it since our first season in 2014.

    30 g jar. Keep it in the fridge at 0 to 4 °C.

  • Bee pollen

    Pollen pellets the bees bring back from their forage. The colour depends on the plants it comes from.

    Packs of 120 g, 200 g and 450 g, or by arrangement.

  • Propolis

    Available as granules.

    Call for quantities and availability.

  • Beeswax

    The bees make the wax themselves and build their comb from it. We cast wax items from it.

    Wax figures and decorations.

  • Gift boxes

    Honey makes a lovely gift. We pack the jars in gift boxes with a honeycomb pattern.

    Boxes for one, two or three jars.

Ask what is available now.
