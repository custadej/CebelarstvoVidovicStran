Awards

More than 15 golds for our honey.

Every award on this page is documented with a certificate. Here they are, year by year.

  • 2019

    National champion for wildflower honey

    Awarded by the Slovenian Beekeepers' Association at the national sensory evaluation of honey. Only honeys that became champions of their type in the evaluations can compete for it.

  • 2019

    Quality mark

    Dobrote slovenskih kmetij awards it to a product rated gold three years in a row. Our wildflower honey from Haloze received it.

  • 2018

    Anton Janša Award, 2nd class

    For special merit in advancing Slovenian beekeeping.
