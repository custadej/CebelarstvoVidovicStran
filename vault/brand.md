# Brand — observed, not yet designed

What exists today (2026-11-14). The new visual direction is decided in [[design-decisions]] after Tadej's OK. Related: [[asset-log]], [[facts]].

## Existing identity

- **Name**: Čebelarstvo Vidovič. Certificates and labels all use exactly this form.
- **Logo**: cartoon bee (big eyes, smile) sitting on a yellow AŽ-style hive, with the wordmark "Čebelarstvo / Vidovič" in a bold black humanist sans, drop shadow. Only source: 497×223 PNG.
- **Jar label**: cream/yellow label with brown outline, logo top-left, honey type in brown ("akacijev") above large outlined "MED". Lid with honeycomb print. The label is on every jar he sells, so the site should not fight it.
- **Tagline** on the old site: "Kar delaš, delaj z ljubeznijo ..."
- **Old site**: Joomla "jp-hope" template, green accent, Verdana/Quicksand. Nothing to carry over visually.

## Tension to resolve with Tadej

The brief asks for a premium, editorial look and to avoid the yellow-and-black bee cliché. The existing logo is exactly a friendly yellow-and-black cartoon bee. Options:

1. Redraw the logo faithfully as SVG and use it small (header/footer, favicon), letting typography carry the premium feel.
2. Use a typographic wordmark on the site and keep the bee logo only where the jar label appears.
3. Propose a refined mark later (out of scope unless asked).

Default until answered: option 1, no redesign.

## Voice (from the brief + his own text)

- First person singular where Jože speaks ("S čebelami sem se prvič srečal ..."), "mi" for the business.
- Warm, plain, local. Short sentences. He already writes this way; keep his phrasing where possible.
- No health promises, no superlatives that are not backed by a certificate.
